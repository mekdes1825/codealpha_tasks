# Task 2: Phishing Awareness Training

## Project Overview

This project is a security-awareness training presentation designed to educate people about phishing attacks, social engineering, and online security.

The presentation explains how to recognize suspicious messages and fake websites, avoid common phishing techniques, and respond safely to potential phishing incidents.

## Objectives

- Explain phishing attacks and how they work.
- Identify suspicious emails and fake websites.
- Understand common social-engineering tactics.
- Learn practical methods to prevent phishing attacks.
- Explore realistic phishing scenarios.
- Test knowledge through interactive quiz questions.

## Topics Covered

1. Introduction to phishing
2. How phishing attacks work
3. Warning signs in phishing emails
4. Identifying fake websites and suspicious links
5. Social-engineering techniques
6. Phishing through email, SMS, calls, and social media
7. Common phishing scenarios
8. Best practices for protection
9. Responding to suspected phishing incidents
10. Knowledge assessment quizzes

## Quiz and Assessment

The presentation includes four quiz questions covering:

- Suspicious account notifications
- Impersonation and urgent payment requests
- Fake website addresses
- Responding to compromised credentials

Each question includes the correct answer and an explanation.

## Learning Outcomes

After completing this training, participants should be able to:

- Recognize common phishing warning signs.
- Verify suspicious requests using trusted channels.
- Protect passwords and authentication codes.
- Respond appropriately to suspected phishing attacks.

## Conclusion

This project promotes cybersecurity awareness by teaching practical techniques to recognize phishing attempts, protect sensitive information, and respond safely to suspicious online activity.

## Ethical Considerations

The examples are intended for educational purposes. The training encourages responsible cybersecurity practices and protection of personal information.
